## 👀 What if an advertisement could understand you before you even say what you want?

This project explores how advertising has evolved from traditional print and broadcast media to social media, mobile advertising and AI-driven personalization.

But this wasn't just a presentation.

We wanted to turn the topic into a **visual story** — and eventually became the advertisers ourselves.
